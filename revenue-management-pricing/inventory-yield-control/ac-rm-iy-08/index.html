Interline and Codeshare Inventory Control

Interline and codeshare inventory is controlled so that partner-sold seats on Air Canada-operated flights are managed within the same optimisation as directly sold inventory.

Air Canada contextA codeshare seat sold by a partner and a seat sold directly both draw from the same physical aircraft, so if either channel is not correctly reflected the optimisation is working from an incomplete picture of true demand.
Trigger
The nightly inventory optimisation cycle runs, incorporating partner sales activity from the prior day.
Outcome
A unified inventory position across direct, GDS, codeshare and interline sales channels, correctly reflected in the optimisation.

Key performance indicators

  • Consolidated before the nightly optimisation run at 100 percent
  • Reconciliation completed for 100 percent of codeshare flights nightly
  • Distribution completed within 2 hours of the optimisation run at 100 percent
  • Consistency confirmed across all channels within 4 hours of distribution

Risks and control points

  • A partner-side booking error showing as available capacity that does not actually exist
  • Distribution latency leaving a partner system selling against outdated availability
  • A codeshare flight showing different availability depending on which carrier's channel is checked
  • Partner sales reporting lags of more than a day feeding a stale position into optimisation
