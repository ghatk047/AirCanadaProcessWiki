Key performance indicators

  • Optimisation completed within the nightly batch window at 100 percent
  • Push completed before the sales day opens at 100 percent
  • Exceptions resolved before the inventory push at 95 percent
  • Consistency confirmed across channels within 2 hours of the push

Risks and control points

  • A failed nightly push leaving a flight selling against stale controls with no automatic alert
  • GDS channel propagation lagging the direct channel by an unmeasured interval
  • Optimisation exceptions concentrating on the same complex routes without a structural fix
  • A bid price near a fare boundary flipping availability in a way that is hard to explain after the fact
