Inventory Feed Reconciliation to Altea

Nightly inventory feeds from PROS to Amadeus Altea are reconciled to confirm every optimisation decision actually took effect in the system passengers book against.

Air Canada contextAn optimisation decision that does not actually reach inventory is invisible until a booking outcome looks wrong, by which point the revenue impact has already occurred.
Trigger
The nightly reconciliation cycle runs after the inventory push completes.
Outcome
A confirmed match between the optimisation output and live inventory, with any discrepancy corrected before the sales day.

Key performance indicators

  • Comparison completed before the sales day opens at 100 percent
  • Discrepancies corrected within 4 hours of detection at 95 percent
  • Zero flights selling against a materially incorrect control for more than 4 hours
  • Report issued daily at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • An optimisation decision failing to reach live inventory with no automatic alert
  • Revenue impact from a missed sync only becoming visible after a booking outcome looks wrong
  • Recurring discrepancy patterns not tracked as a trend across days, hiding a systemic feed issue
  • Legitimate in-flight bookings during the batch window complicating discrepancy attribution
