Group Booking Evaluation and Quote

Group booking requests are evaluated for viability and quoted with terms and pricing appropriate to block size, lead time and displacement of individual passenger revenue.

Air Canada contextA group quote is fundamentally a displacement calculation: the group's value has to be weighed against the individual passenger revenue it will block from the same seats.
Trigger
A group booking enquiry is received through sales or the group desk.
Outcome
A quoted group booking with agreed terms, or a documented decline where the group would displace more revenue than it contributes.

Key performance indicators

  • Availability assessed within 2 business days at 100 percent
  • Displacement calculated for 100 percent of viable requests
  • Quotations issued within 3 business days of request at 95 percent
  • Confirmed group revenue meeting or exceeding the displacement cost at 90 percent

Risks and control points

  • Competitive pressure pushing a quote below the calculated minimum without a documented override
  • An unconfirmed hold blocking individual inventory well past its firming deadline
  • Displacement estimation on a high-demand flight being highly sensitive to forecast error
  • Group demand and individual optimisation competing for the same seats with no shared arbitration rule
