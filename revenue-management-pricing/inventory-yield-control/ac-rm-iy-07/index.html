Award Seat Inventory Allocation for Aeroplan

Award seat inventory for Aeroplan redemptions is allocated by flight and class, balancing member redemption value against the cash revenue the same seats could otherwise generate.

Air Canada contextAward seats are not free capacity: every seat allocated to redemption is a seat the cash revenue optimisation does not get to sell, so the two processes are directly in tension by design.
Trigger
The recurring award inventory allocation cycle runs, or a redemption demand spike prompts an off-cycle review.
Outcome
An award seat allocation by flight and class that balances redemption availability against protected cash revenue.

Key performance indicators

  • Demand estimated for 100 percent of routes each cycle
  • Released before the redemption sales window opens at 100 percent
  • Allocation calculated for 100 percent of eligible flights each cycle
  • Member redemption satisfaction score maintained above target

Risks and control points

  • Award seats allocated against protected cash revenue with no single objectively correct trade-off
  • A release failure appearing to the member as simply no availability, with no diagnostic trail
  • Redemption search activity overstating true demand and skewing the allocation calculation
  • Persistent low uptake on an allocated route not feeding back into the next allocation cycle
