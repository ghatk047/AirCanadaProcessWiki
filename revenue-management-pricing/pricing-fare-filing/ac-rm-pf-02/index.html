Real Time Dynamic Pricing Model Governance

PROS real-time dynamic pricing models are governed to confirm the willingness-to-pay algorithms remain aligned with commercial intent and do not drift into unintended pricing behaviour.

Air Canada contextA dynamic pricing model optimises against the objective it is given, and a subtle misconfiguration can produce pricing that is locally optimal but commercially wrong before anyone notices.
Trigger
The recurring model governance review cycle runs, or an anomalous pricing pattern is flagged for investigation.
Outcome
A validated dynamic pricing model operating within approved bounds, with any drift identified and corrected.

Key performance indicators

  • Screening run daily at 100 percent
  • Root cause identified within 3 business days of flagging
  • Governance decision taken within 5 business days of investigation at 100 percent
  • 100 percent of governance decisions documented with rationale

Risks and control points

  • A subtle model misconfiguration producing locally optimal but commercially wrong pricing
  • Anomaly thresholds tuned against false positives missing a genuine slow drift
  • A constraint fixing one anomaly creating an unintended effect elsewhere in the network
  • Undocumented parameter changes accumulating until model behaviour is no longer explainable
