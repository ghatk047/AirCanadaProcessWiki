Fare Construction and Filing to ATPCO

New and amended fares are constructed and filed to ATPCO, covering base fare levels, routing and fare basis codes across the network.

Air Canada contextATPCO filing is the slowest link in an otherwise fast pricing chain: dynamic pricing can react within the day, but a filed fare change propagates on ATPCO's distribution cycle.
Trigger
A pricing decision requires a new fare or an amendment to an existing filed fare.
Outcome
A correctly filed fare visible and sellable across every distribution channel that consumes ATPCO data.

Key performance indicators

  • Filings submitted within the required lead time at 100 percent
  • Propagation confirmed within 24 hours of the effective date at 100 percent
  • Test bookings verified for 100 percent of material new filings
  • Fare filing errors caught before material revenue impact above 95 percent

Risks and control points

  • An unintended conflict with an existing fare only discovered after filing
  • Channels consuming the ATPCO feed on different cycles, leaving inconsistent visibility
  • A pricing error selling seats at the wrong fare before an anomaly is caught
  • Test coverage unable to span every fare and routing combination a new filing enables
