Corporate and Negotiated Fare Administration

Negotiated corporate fares are administered for contracted accounts, covering discount structure, eligibility validation and compliance monitoring against the negotiated terms.

Air Canada contextA negotiated corporate fare is a contract enforced through system configuration, so a configuration error either leaks a discount to ineligible bookers or denies it to eligible ones.
Trigger
A corporate account contract is signed or renewed, or a compliance review identifies a discrepancy.
Outcome
Correctly configured corporate fares available only to eligible bookers, with usage monitored against contracted volume commitments.

Key performance indicators

  • Configured within 5 business days of contract signing at 100 percent
  • Access restricted correctly at 100 percent of configuration
  • Tracked monthly for 100 percent of active corporate contracts
  • Audited quarterly for 100 percent of material corporate accounts

Risks and control points

  • Contract terms losing nuance when translated into fare rule logic
  • A superseded discount remaining active past its contract term because configuration was not updated
  • Fare leakage through an indirect booking channel being harder to detect than direct misuse
  • Volume shortfall against a commercial commitment not being escalated promptly
