Competitive Fare Response and Match Decision

Competitive fare positions are monitored and a match or hold decision is made within defined delegation limits, balancing share protection against margin erosion.

Air Canada contextA fare match decision has to be made faster than the full commercial review cycle can run, so delegated authority within defined limits is what makes a timely response possible at all.
Trigger
A competitor files a fare change on an overlapping route.
Outcome
A documented match or hold decision issued within the competitive response window.

Key performance indicators

  • Changes detected within 4 hours of filing at 100 percent
  • Decisions within delegated limits made within 1 business day at 90 percent
  • Escalations resolved within 2 business days at 90 percent
  • Competitive response filed within 2 business days of detection at 85 percent

Risks and control points

  • A decision requiring escalation outlasting the competitive window it was meant to address
  • Detection missing a competitor change filed through a channel outside the monitoring feed
  • Share impact modelling assuming a competitor fare sells at a volume it may not actually achieve
  • A filed match still subject to the same ATPCO propagation delay as any standard fare change
