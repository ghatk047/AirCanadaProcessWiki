Tax Fee and Surcharge Maintenance

Government-mandated taxes, fees and carrier surcharges are maintained across every market Air Canada serves, kept current with changing regulatory rates.

Air Canada contextTax and fee maintenance is unglamorous but has zero tolerance for error: undercharging is a direct revenue loss recovered from the airline, and overcharging is a customer-facing compliance problem.
Trigger
A government or airport authority changes a tax, fee or charge rate, or a scheduled periodic review runs.
Outcome
Correctly filed and applied taxes, fees and surcharges across all affected fares and markets.

Key performance indicators

  • Changes identified within 5 business days of the effective notice at 100 percent
  • Updated before the change's effective date at 95 percent
  • Verified for 100 percent of material rate changes
  • Reconciled monthly for 100 percent of applicable taxes

Risks and control points

  • A short-notice jurisdiction rate change taking effect before the filing is updated
  • A filing error compounding across every booking made before it is caught
  • A rate applying inconsistently across fare types and channels in a way that is easy to miss
  • Notification channels varying by jurisdiction with no central aggregation point
