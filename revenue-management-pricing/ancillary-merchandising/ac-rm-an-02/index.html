Checked Baggage Fee Management

Checked baggage fees are managed across fare families, markets and Altitude tiers, reflecting included-baggage entitlements and market-specific competitive positioning.

Air Canada contextBaggage fee logic intersects fare family, market, and loyalty tier simultaneously, which makes it one of the more complex ancillary configurations to get right and to keep right.
Trigger
The recurring baggage fee review cycle runs, or a competitive baggage policy change requires reassessment.
Outcome
Correctly filed baggage fees honouring fare family inclusions and Altitude entitlements, competitive in each market.

Revenue Management & Pricing

Key performance indicators

  • Inclusion matrix current for 100 percent of active fare families
  • Validated for 100 percent of Altitude tiers before filing
  • Verified consistent between booking and check-in at 100 percent
  • Baggage fee dispute volume below target threshold

Risks and control points

  • A fee charged correctly at booking but incorrectly at check-in creating a recurring dispute pattern
  • Entitled bag allowances by Altitude tier not correctly reflected at the point of check-in
  • Fare family definitions changing independently of the baggage fee review cycle
  • A structure competitive in one market being out of step with local expectations in another
