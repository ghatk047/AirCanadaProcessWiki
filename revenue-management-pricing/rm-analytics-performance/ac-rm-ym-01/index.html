Key performance indicators

  • Review completed before market open for 100 percent of assigned routes
  • Exceptions actioned within the trading day at 90 percent
  • Reviewed daily for 100 percent of standing overrides
  • Scanned daily for 100 percent of the assigned portfolio

Risks and control points

  • Portfolio size exceeding what a thorough manual review can genuinely cover each day
  • An override applied for a since-resolved situation persisting unnoticed in a busy review queue
  • Exception volume spikes around disruption events crowding out routine daily review
  • Undocumented context being lost between analysts during a portfolio handover
