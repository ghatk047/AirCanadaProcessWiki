Forecast Accuracy and Model Drift Monitoring

Forecast accuracy is tracked and model drift is monitored across the demand forecasting engine, identifying when a model's error pattern has moved beyond normal variance.

Air Canada contextForecast error is expected within a normal range; the discipline here is distinguishing ordinary noise from a genuine, sustained drift that means the model itself needs retuning.
Trigger
The recurring model accuracy monitoring cycle runs, or a sustained accuracy decline is flagged.
Outcome
A forecast accuracy report with any drifting model or market segment identified for model team action.

Key performance indicators

  • Measured for 100 percent of market segments each cycle
  • Flagged within one monitoring cycle of drift beginning at 90 percent
  • Escalated within 2 business days of confirmation at 100 percent
  • Confirmed drift cases resolved within one model release cycle

Risks and control points

  • Accuracy at different booking horizons moving in opposite directions and masking each other in aggregate
  • The normal variance range itself going stale as the network changes around it
  • Drift root cause being an external market shift rather than an actual model defect
  • Model retuning cadence lagging the urgency of a confirmed drift finding
