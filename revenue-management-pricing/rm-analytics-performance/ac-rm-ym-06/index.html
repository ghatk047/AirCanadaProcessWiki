Pricing and RM Data Feed to the Lakehouse

Revenue management and pricing data feeds are validated and delivered into the Databricks lakehouse, providing the grounding data for analytics and machine learning use cases across the function.

Air Canada contextEvery model and dashboard downstream of revenue management is only as trustworthy as this pipeline, which makes data quality validation here a control point rather than a routine data engineering task.
Trigger
The recurring data pipeline cycle runs, or a schema or source system change requires pipeline validation.
Outcome
Validated, current revenue management data available in the lakehouse for downstream analytics and AI model use.

Key performance indicators

  • Pipelines complete within the scheduled window at 99 percent
  • Validated for 100 percent of scheduled runs
  • Remediated within 1 business day of detection at 90 percent
  • Confirmed within the scheduled window at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • A source system schema change silently breaking a downstream pipeline transformation
  • Quality rules covering known failure modes but missing an entirely new class of data issue
  • Remediation depending on a source system fix outside the data team's direct control
  • Downstream model and dashboard consumers not being notified when data availability is delayed
