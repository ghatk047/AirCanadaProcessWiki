Key performance indicators

  • Case registration time < 24 hours
  • Cases assigned within 48 hours of receipt
  • Data compilation within 72 hours
  • Assessment completed within 10 days
  • Resolution implemented within 10 days of plan preparation

Risks and control points

  • High backlog of APPR complaints impacting case resolution times
  • Complex regulations leading to prolonged review processes
  • Technical issues with external systems causing submission delays
  • Delays in compliance reviews affecting overall case timelines
  • Inconsistent communication plans leading to customer dissatisfaction
