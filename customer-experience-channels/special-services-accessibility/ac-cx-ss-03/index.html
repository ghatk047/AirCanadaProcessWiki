Unaccompanied Minor Handling

An unaccompanied minor is accepted, supervised and handed off through the journey, from parent or guardian check-in through receiving-party verification at arrival.

Air Canada contextChain of custody is the defining requirement of this process: at no point in the journey can there be ambiguity about who is responsible for the child's safety.
Trigger
A booking is made for an unaccompanied minor, or the child arrives at the airport for check-in.
Outcome
The unaccompanied minor safely delivered to a verified receiving party at destination, with supervision maintained throughout the journey.

Key performance indicators

  • Verified before acceptance for 100 percent of unaccompanied minor bookings
  • Continuous supervision maintained for 100 percent of unaccompanied minor connections
  • Identity verified before handoff for 100 percent of arrivals
  • Zero chain of custody gaps recorded

Risks and control points

  • A connection at a hub with tight timing being the highest-risk point for a supervision gap
  • A receiving party who cannot be verified requiring an escalation path that avoids leaving the child unsupervised
  • An unclosed custody record leaving ambiguity about whether handoff actually completed correctly
  • Documentation requirements differing by destination, particularly complicating international travel
