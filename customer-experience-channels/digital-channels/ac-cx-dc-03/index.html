Key performance indicators

  • Scan events captured for 95 percent of handling milestones
  • Status updated within 5 minutes of each scan event at 90 percent
  • Anomalies flagged within 30 minutes of a missed expected scan at 85 percent
  • Customer contact volume for bag status enquiries reduced against pre-launch baseline

Risks and control points

  • Manual handling steps at some stations not consistently generating the scan event the app depends on
  • A missing scan leaving the customer's tracking view stale or showing an inaccurate status
  • A missing final scan showing a bag as in transit after the customer has already collected it
  • Anomaly detection tuning that is too loose or too tight relative to genuine mishandling
