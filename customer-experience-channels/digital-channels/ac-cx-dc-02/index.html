Mobile App Check In and Boarding Pass Issue

A customer checks in and receives a boarding pass through the mobile app, from eligibility opening through boarding pass issuance.

Air Canada contextMobile check-in has to work reliably at scale in the exact hour before a bank of departures, which makes it one of the highest-consequence peak-load moments in the digital estate.
Trigger
Online check-in opens for a booked flight, typically 24 hours before departure.
Outcome
An issued digital boarding pass, with the customer checked in and any applicable seat or bag selection completed.

Key performance indicators

  • Check-in completed within 5 seconds at 97 percent
  • Boarding pass issued within 5 seconds of check-in completion at 98 percent
  • Eligibility correctly assessed for 100 percent of check-in attempts
  • Peak-hour check-in system availability at 99.9 percent

Risks and control points

  • Peak check-in volume in the hour before a large departure bank straining system response time
  • A boarding pass failing to generate, leaving the customer with no proof of check-in
  • Document requirement logic not staying current with destination-specific changes
  • A boarding pass visible on one device but not synced to another, causing gate confusion
