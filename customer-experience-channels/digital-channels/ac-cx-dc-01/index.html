Key performance indicators

  • Search results returned within 3 seconds at 95 percent
  • Payment processed within 5 seconds at 98 percent
  • Booking completion rate from search to confirmation meeting target
  • Confirmation delivered within 2 minutes of issuance at 98 percent

Risks and control points

  • Payment gateway timeouts during peak periods being the leading cause of abandoned bookings
  • Too many sequential ancillary prompts measurably reducing overall booking completion
  • A confirmation delay after payment capture creating real customer anxiety and contact volume
  • Strict field validation abandoning otherwise valid bookings at the details capture step
