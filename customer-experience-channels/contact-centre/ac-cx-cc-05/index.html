Key performance indicators

  • 95% accuracy in capacity trend analysis
  • 100% identification of peak travel days
  • 80% forecast accuracy within a 5% margin

Risks and control points

  • Inaccurate historical data leading to poor forecasting
  • Failed recruitment due to external factors like visa issues
  • Overreliance on overtime leading to staff burnout
  • Delayed service level monitoring causing customer dissatisfaction
  • Insufficient management response time during crises
