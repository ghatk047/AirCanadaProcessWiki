Virtual Assistant Conversation Handling

Conversations with the Air Canada virtual assistant are handled and escalated to a human agent when the assistant's confidence or authority is insufficient to resolve the request safely.

Air Canada contextEvery response the assistant gives is subject to the human-in-loop governance established in AC-IT-AI-03, following the Moffatt ruling. The escalation threshold here is a compliance control, not just a service design choice.
Trigger
A customer initiates a chat conversation with the virtual assistant through the website or app.
Outcome
A resolved conversation within the assistant's governed scope, or a clean escalation to a human agent with context preserved.

Key performance indicators

  • 100 percent of responses grounded within the approved source set
  • Below-threshold responses escalated at 100 percent
  • Context preserved for 100 percent of escalations at handoff
  • Zero confirmed cases of the assistant stating an incorrect policy position

Risks and control points

  • A response drawing on unapproved or outdated content, repeating the Moffatt failure mode
  • An escalation threshold set too low, letting an uncertain answer reach the customer directly
  • Conversation history not transferring cleanly at escalation, forcing the customer to restart
  • Ambiguous customer phrasing producing lower-confidence interpretations more often than clear requests
