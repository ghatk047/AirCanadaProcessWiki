Volcanic Ash and Space Weather Response

A volcanic ash advisory or space weather event affecting the network is assessed and an operational response, including route avoidance or altitude restriction, is coordinated across affected flights.

Air Canada contextThese events are rare but high-consequence, and the response has to move faster than the standard planning cycle, which means a defined escalation path matters more here than in routine operations.
Trigger
A volcanic ash advisory or significant space weather event is issued affecting Air Canada's network.
Outcome
An operational response protecting affected flights, with route or altitude adjustments coordinated across every affected flight in the network.
Domain
Flight Operations & Dispatch

Key performance indicators

  • Assessed within 30 minutes of advisory issuance at 100 percent
  • Identified within 1 hour of assessment at 100 percent
  • Determined for 100 percent of affected flights before their departure or continued operation
  • Zero flights operated in violation of an active volcanic ash or space weather restriction

Risks and control points

  • A widespread event affecting flights across multiple simultaneous phases of flight at once
  • A route adjustment for one flight creating a knock-on capacity constraint on an adjacent airway
  • Advisory information quality and update frequency varying by the issuing authority
  • Response coordination needing to move faster than the standard planning communication cadence
