Operational Flight Plan Construction

An operational flight plan is constructed in NetLine Ops for each flight, covering route, altitude, fuel and alternate airport selection before dispatch release.

Air Canada contextThe flight plan is the technical foundation every subsequent dispatch decision builds on; an error here propagates into fuel policy, alternate selection and ultimately the dispatch release itself.
Trigger
A flight's dispatch preparation window opens ahead of scheduled departure.
Outcome
A complete, validated operational flight plan ready for dispatch release and crew briefing.

Key performance indicators

  • Fuel calculated within regulatory reserve requirements at 100 percent
  • Alternates selected meeting regulatory minimums at 100 percent
  • Filed within the required lead time before departure at 100 percent
  • Plan revision rate due to late weather change tracked against target

Risks and control points

  • Fuel calculation depending on weather data that can change materially before departure
  • Marginal destination weather requiring an alternate that adds meaningful fuel burden late in planning
  • A plan validated against data that goes stale before it is actually used for dispatch release
  • Performance limit verification at a high-elevation or short-runway destination requiring careful margin checks
