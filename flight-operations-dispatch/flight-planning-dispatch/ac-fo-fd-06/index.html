Flight Plan Filing to NAV CANADA

The final loadsheet is issued to the flight crew, confirming the aircraft's actual weight and balance for departure, and any last-minute change is incorporated before off-block.

Air Canada contextThe loadsheet is the last document in the chain before the aircraft can leave the gate, so it is also where every upstream planning error, if any survived this far, becomes visible.
Trigger
The load plan is finalised and ready for loadsheet issuance ahead of departure.
Outcome
A confirmed loadsheet delivered to the flight crew, with the aircraft cleared to depart within its weight and balance limits.

Key performance indicators

  • Delivered before off-block for 100 percent of flights
  • Acknowledged before pushback clearance at 100 percent
  • Revised loadsheet issued within 5 minutes of a material change at 90 percent
  • Zero departures with an unacknowledged final loadsheet

Risks and control points

  • Datalink delivery being degraded at some stations, delaying loadsheet receipt
  • An unacknowledged loadsheet being assumed received rather than actively followed up
  • A last-minute weight change after departure preparation has begun requiring urgent recalculation
  • Every upstream planning error, if any survived this far, becoming visible only at this final step
