Key performance indicators

  • Diversion airports identified for 100 percent of ETOPS sectors
  • Calculated within the aircraft's certified ETOPS rating at 100 percent
  • Validated before dispatch release for 100 percent of ETOPS flights
  • Clearance obtained before entering oceanic airspace at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • A diversion airport becoming unsuitable due to weather or operational status after planning completes
  • A late change to diversion airport status not triggering a full ETOPS re-validation
  • Communication procedure requirements varying by oceanic region in ways easy to miss
  • Oceanic clearance timing depending on coordination across multiple air traffic control authorities
