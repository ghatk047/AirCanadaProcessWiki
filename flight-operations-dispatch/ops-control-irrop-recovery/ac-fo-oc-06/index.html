Key performance indicators

  • Time to detection: < 10 minutes
  • Time to impact assessment: < 20 minutes
  • Time to ground stop: < 30 minutes
  • Time to rerouting: < 45 minutes
  • Time to rescheduling: < 60 minutes

Risks and control points

  • Inaccurate system status updates leading to delayed recovery actions.
  • Manual detection of outages causing significant delays.
  • Inconsistent communication protocols leading to operational confusion.
  • Limited alternative routes affecting flight efficiency.
  • Regulatory constraints delaying rescheduling processes.
