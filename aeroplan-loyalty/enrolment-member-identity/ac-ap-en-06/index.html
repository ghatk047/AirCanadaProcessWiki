Member Data Privacy Request Handling

A member's request to access, correct or delete their personal data under PIPEDA or Quebec Law 25 is processed within the regulated response timeframe.

Air Canada contextThis is a hard regulatory deadline, not a service level target: Law 25 in particular imposes specific timeframes and documentation obligations that do not flex for operational convenience.
Trigger
A member submits a data access, correction or deletion request through any channel.
Outcome
The member's request fulfilled within the regulated timeframe, with a documented audit trail of the action taken.

Key performance indicators

  • Identity verified before any data action at 100 percent
  • Data location completed within the regulatory response window at 100 percent
  • Action executed within the regulatory deadline at 100 percent
  • Response issued within the regulatory deadline at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • Member personal data being distributed across systems with no single index to locate it completely
  • A missed regulatory deadline constituting a compliance failure regardless of the underlying action
  • Deletion conflicting with a legitimate retention obligation such as an unresolved financial record
  • Insufficiently robust verification allowing someone other than the member to action the request
