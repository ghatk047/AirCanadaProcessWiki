Key performance indicators

  • Re-confirmed for 100 percent of partner redemptions before booking
  • Both systems reflecting the booking within 15 minutes at 95 percent
  • Settled within the same transaction as booking issuance at 100 percent
  • Partner redemption failure rate below target threshold

Risks and control points

  • A partner seat sold by the partner carrier before Air Canada's booking transaction completes
  • A booking succeeding in one carrier's system and failing in the other, requiring manual reconciliation
  • Partner availability data freshness being entirely outside Air Canada's own control
  • Interline settlement running on a cycle separate from the member-facing points deduction
