eUpgrade Redemption and Clearance

A member requests a reversal of a points redemption, whether for a cancelled award booking or a redemption made in error, restoring points to the member's ledger.

Air Canada contextA reversal has to unwind two systems together, the booking and the points ledger, and a partial reversal that only completes one side leaves the member either shortchanged or with an unintended double benefit.
Trigger
A member requests to cancel or reverse a completed redemption.
Outcome
A correctly reversed redemption with points restored to the member's ledger and any associated booking cancelled.

Key performance indicators

  • Eligibility determined within 1 business day of request at 100 percent
  • Points restored within 5 business days of cancellation at 100 percent
  • Fee calculated correctly against the applicable fare rule at 100 percent
  • Reversal-related member complaint rate below target threshold

Risks and control points

  • A partial reversal completing on the booking side but not the points side, or the reverse
  • A ledger discrepancy where the restored amount does not exactly match the original deduction
  • Fee calculation misapplying a fare rule not originally designed with reversal in mind
  • A booking with one segment already flown complicating what a clean cancellation actually means
