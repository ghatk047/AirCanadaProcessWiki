Points Plus Cash Redemption

A member redeems points to clear an upgrade to a higher cabin on an eligible booking, subject to fare class eligibility and upgrade inventory clearance rules.

Air Canada contexteUpgrade clearance depends on premium cabin inventory that is also being actively managed by revenue management for paid upgrades, so the two processes compete for the same seats.
Trigger
A member requests an eUpgrade against an eligible existing booking.
Outcome
A confirmed cabin upgrade with points deducted, or a documented reason the upgrade could not clear.

Key performance indicators

  • Eligibility verified within seconds of the request at 100 percent
  • Waitlist priority applied correctly by Altitude tier at 100 percent
  • Points deducted only upon confirmed clearance at 100 percent
  • eUpgrade clearance rate meeting target for Super Elite and top-tier members

Risks and control points

  • eUpgrade and paid upgrade clearance competing for the same premium cabin inventory with no shared arbitration
  • Points deducted before clearance is confirmed, risking loss for an upgrade that never clears
  • Waitlist priority logic being difficult for a member to understand when a lower-tier member appears to clear first
  • Fare class eligibility rules not being intuitive to the member making the request
