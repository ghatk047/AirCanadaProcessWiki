Key performance indicators

  • Average time to resolve a dispute < 48 hours
  • Dispute resolution accuracy rate > 95%
  • Member satisfaction with dispute resolution process > 80%
  • Number of disputes escalated to duty manager per month < 10
  • System uptime for Aeroplan Platform and SAP Customer Data Cloud > 99.9%

Risks and control points

  • Delays in receiving dispute notifications from partners
  • Inaccurate data entry or analysis leading to incorrect root cause identification
  • Technical failures in email/SMS systems causing notification delays
  • Manual closure errors in Aeroplan Platform
  • Financial discrepancies due to delayed resolution reporting
