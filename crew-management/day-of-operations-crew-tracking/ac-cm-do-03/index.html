Key performance indicators

  • Time to acknowledge disruption < 5 minutes
  • Initial impact assessment completed within 10 minutes
  • Notifications sent within 5 minutes of impact assessment
  • Affected flight list generated within 15 minutes
  • Roster correction completed within 10 minutes

Risks and control points

  • Inaccurate roster data leading to incorrect reassignments.
  • Union compliance checks delaying approval.
  • Manual corrections being time-consuming and error-prone.
  • No valid reassignments requiring union escalation.
  • Delays in crew notifications causing further disruption.
