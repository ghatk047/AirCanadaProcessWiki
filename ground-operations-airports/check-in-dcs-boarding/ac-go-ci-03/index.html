Advance Passenger Information Transmission

Advance passenger information is transmitted to CBSA, CATSA and destination-country authorities ahead of departure, meeting the specific timing and data requirements of each regulator.

Air Canada contextDifferent regulators have different timing and data requirements for the same passenger, which means this process has to satisfy several independent, non-negotiable compliance regimes simultaneously rather than a single unified standard.
Trigger
A passenger completes check-in for a flight requiring advance passenger information transmission.
Outcome
Complete, accurate advance passenger information transmitted within each regulator's required timeframe, clearing the passenger for travel.

Key performance indicators

  • Compiled for 100 percent of applicable flights at check-in completion
  • Transmitted within each regulator's required window at 100 percent
  • Confirmed before boarding for 100 percent of applicable passengers
  • Zero flights delayed due to a missed regulatory transmission deadline

Risks and control points

  • Different destination regulators having different transmission timing requirements for the same flight
  • Data completeness depending on the accuracy of a document scan or manual entry at check-in
  • A clearance hold received close to boarding time compressing the resolution window before departure
  • CATSA and CBSA requirements being distinct despite substantially overlapping underlying data
