Key performance indicators

  • Zero checks missed due to unavailable ground time
  • Ground time embedded for 100 percent of forecast checks
  • Base capacity sufficient for all embedded slots at 100 percent
  • Unplanned removal risk assessed for 100 percent of near-due tails

Risks and control points

  • Maintenance ground time traded away under schedule pressure, producing an unplanned removal later
  • Multiple tails converging on the same base capacity in the same window
  • Utilisation running ahead of plan and pulling a check date forward unpredictably
  • Embedding a long check slot forcing rebuild of an otherwise efficient rotation
