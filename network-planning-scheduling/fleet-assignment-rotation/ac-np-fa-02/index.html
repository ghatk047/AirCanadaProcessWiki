Key performance indicators

  • Rotations closing at a maintenance base at 100 percent
  • Zero rotations exceeding a check interval at commitment
  • Turn times meeting station minimums at 100 percent
  • Rotation compatible with crew basing above 90 percent
  • Infeasible rotations resolved before freeze at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • A rotation that never returns its tail to a maintenance base within the check interval
  • Ground time built to the station minimum with no buffer for routine delay
  • Rotation and crew base optimisation happening in separate systems, producing conflicting outcomes
  • In-season fleet swaps invalidating rotations without full re-validation
