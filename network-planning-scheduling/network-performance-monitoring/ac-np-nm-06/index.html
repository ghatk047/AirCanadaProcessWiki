Schedule Reliability and Block Time Tuning

Key performance indicators

  • Distribution compiled for 100 percent of routes each review cycle
  • Flagged routes identified each review cycle at 100 percent
  • Feasibility assessed for 100 percent of proposed changes
  • Chronic block time variance reduced season over season on tuned routes

Risks and control points

  • Tuning to an average that still leaves a route chronically late in one direction on a windy sector
  • A block time increase on one leg breaking the rotation it is embedded in
  • Seasonal wind effects masked by an annual average and misread as a planning error
  • Correction only taking effect at the next schedule build, with no immediate remedy
