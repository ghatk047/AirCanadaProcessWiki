Key performance indicators

  • Material changes flagged within 3 business days of filing
  • Impact assessed within 5 business days of flagging
  • Response recommendation issued within 10 business days of a material change
  • Tracking refreshed weekly for all overlapping markets

Risks and control points

  • Filed competitor schedules not operating as filed, overstating the real competitive threat
  • Materiality thresholds applied inconsistently, so a real threat goes unflagged
  • Findings captured per incident rather than consolidated into a standing competitive picture
  • Tactical and structural response recommendations competing for the same limited planning capacity
