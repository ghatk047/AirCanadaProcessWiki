Connection Bank Integrity Monitoring

The integrity of connecting banks is monitored in service to confirm the connections sold actually achieve their planned reliability, and to feed structural findings back into bank design.

Air Canada contextNot every misconnect is the same kind of problem: an occasional weather-driven misconnect is a different issue from a connection that is structurally too tight to work reliably, and only the second belongs back in bank design.
Trigger
The recurring bank integrity reporting cycle runs, or a specific connecting market shows a misconnect spike.
Outcome
A bank integrity report identifying connections that are structurally unreliable, distinct from those affected by a one-off disruption.

Key performance indicators

  • Compiled within 5 business days of period close at 100 percent
  • Classification completed for 100 percent of material misconnect markets
  • Findings fed into the next seasonal build at 100 percent
  • Structural misconnect rate reduced season over season on flagged markets

Risks and control points

  • Misconnect data spanning mainline, Rouge and partner-operated legs inconsistently
  • A connection sold as reliable that is actually structurally too tight to work
  • The feedback loop from in-service finding to bank redesign taking a full season
  • One-off disruption misclassified as a structural finding, triggering an unnecessary redesign
