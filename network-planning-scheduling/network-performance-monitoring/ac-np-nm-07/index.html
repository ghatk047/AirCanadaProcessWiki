Network Post Implementation Review

A structured post-implementation review is conducted after each seasonal schedule closes, comparing the delivered schedule against the plan and feeding disciplined lessons into the next build cycle.

Air Canada contextWithout a structured review, lessons from a season live only in the memory of the planners who worked it, and turnover quietly erodes institutional knowledge about what actually worked.
Trigger
A seasonal schedule closes at season end.
Outcome
A documented post-implementation review with findings assigned to a specific owner and traceable into the next planning cycle.

Key performance indicators

  • Reconciliation completed within 20 business days of season end
  • 100 percent of material findings assigned an owner
  • Findings synthesised within 30 business days of season end
  • Recurring findings from the prior season correctly identified as recurring

Risks and control points

  • In-season changes accumulating until the delivered schedule differs substantially from the frozen plan
  • The same structural issue being rediscovered every season with no year-over-year comparison
  • Findings without a named owner and target cycle defaulting to being lost
  • Institutional knowledge about what worked in a season eroding with planner turnover
