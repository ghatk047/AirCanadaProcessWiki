On Time Performance Root Cause Attribution

On-time performance is analysed to root cause, separating network design, airport process, crew and weather contributions so that structural fixes can be distinguished from operational ones.

Air Canada contextOn-time performance is reported as a single number but its causes sit in different domains, so a network-design problem, an airport turnaround problem and a crew problem can produce an identical OTP figure while needing completely different fixes.
Trigger
The recurring OTP reporting cycle runs, or a sustained OTP decline triggers a deep-dive review.
Outcome
An OTP root cause report attributing delay to network, airport, crew, weather or aircraft causes, with recommendations addressed to the right owner.

Key performance indicators

  • Compiled within 3 business days of period close at 100 percent
  • Findings routed within 10 business days of the reporting cycle
  • Network attribution completed each reporting cycle
  • OTP root cause correctly attributed on 85 percent of material delay drivers

Risks and control points

  • Insufficient scheduled ground time reading as an airport delay unless explicitly traced back
  • Crew-caused and maintenance-caused delay coded interchangeably at the point of entry
  • A structural finding routed to the wrong owner going unactioned for a full reporting cycle
  • Station-level process issues masked by a network-level aggregate OTP figure
