Seasonal Schedule Build in NetLine Plan

The Seasonal Schedule Build in NetLine Plan process involves creating and optimizing airline schedules for the upcoming season. The process integrates with various systems to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, optimize operational efficiency, and align with strategic partnerships like the A++ Atlantic Joint Venture.

Air Canada contextThe process is heavily influenced by the A++ Atlantic Joint Venture (A++ JV) partnership, which requires coordinated schedules across Air Canada, United, and Lufthansa Group. Additionally, Toronto Pearson's design around Sixth Freedom traffic adds complexity in balancing connection reliability with aircraft utilization on tight margins.
Trigger
Initiation by the Network Planning & Scheduling team at the start of a new planning cycle, typically six months before the season begins.
Outcome
A fully optimized seasonal schedule that is compliant with all regulatory requirements and has been approved for implementation by senior management.
