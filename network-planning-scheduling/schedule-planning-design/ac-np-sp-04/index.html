Hub Bank Structure Design for YUL and YVR

The process for designing the hub bank structure for YUL (Toronto Pearson) and YVR (Vancouver International) involves complex scheduling and network planning to optimize connectivity and aircraft utilization while adhering to regulatory constraints and joint venture agreements.

Air Canada contextToronto Pearson's Sixth Freedom connecting traffic between the US and Europe/Asia over Canada requires careful balance in connection reliability against aircraft utilisation on tight margins. The A++ Atlantic joint venture with United and Lufthansa Group necessitates coordinated schedules and revenue sharing, adding complexity to transatlantic capacity decisions.
Trigger
A change in demand forecasts, regulatory updates, or operational changes at either YUL or YVR that require a reevaluation of the current hub bank structure.
Outcome
The successful design and implementation of an optimized hub bank structure for both YUL and YVR, ensuring efficient connectivity and aircraft utilization while meeting regulatory requirements and joint venture agreements.
