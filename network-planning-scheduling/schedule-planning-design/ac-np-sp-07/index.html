Rouge Leisure Schedule Integration

Air Canada Rouge operates leisure routes under a separate air operator certificate with a different cabin configuration and cost structure. Its schedule is integrated with the mainline plan so that capacity is placed on the right operating platform without breaking connectivity.

Air Canada contextRouge shares the mainline IT backbone but runs a distinct operational configuration: separate air operator certificate, different cabin product and a different crew cost base. That creates a real process seam. Product and fare configuration diverge on a shared passenger service system, so a passenger connecting from a mainline flight onto a Rouge flight experiences two products under one brand. Rouge was also swept up in the June 2023 mainline outage, which is a reminder that operational separation does not imply IT independence.
Trigger
Seasonal schedule build reaches operator allocation, or a route's seasonal demand profile shifts enough to justify moving it between mainline and Rouge.
Outcome
An integrated schedule with each leg allocated to the appropriate operating platform, with cabin configuration, connectivity and crew implications resolved.

Key performance indicators

  • Platform allocation supported by comparative economics on 100 percent of decisions
  • Rouge feed aligned to mainline waves on 85 percent of connections
  • Cabin and fare configuration correct at first publication above 95 percent
  • Operating carrier correct at publication at 100 percent
  • Maintenance opportunity protected on Rouge rotations at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • Product and fare configuration diverging on a shared passenger service system and reaching the customer as an inconsistent experience under one brand
  • Separate air operator certificate preventing crew interchange when either platform is short
  • Rouge timings optimised for leisure demand missing the mainline connecting wave
  • Operating carrier misdesignation misdirecting regulatory responsibility for the flight
  • Rouge integrated late in the planning cycle, so its constraints surface after mainline is settled
