Schedule Publication and SSIM Distribution

The Schedule Publication and SSIM Distribution process involves creating, validating, and disseminating flight schedules to various stakeholders across Air Canada's network operations, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements and operational constraints.

Air Canada contextThis process is critical for maintaining Toronto Pearson's Sixth Freedom connectivity traffic between the US and Europe/Asia over Canada. The A++ Atlantic Joint Venture with United and Lufthansa Group adds complexity as transatlantic capacity decisions require coordination across multiple partners. Additionally, regulatory constraints such as EU-95 noise abatement rules must be strictly adhered to, impacting schedule design.
Trigger
Completion of the weekly long-range schedule design in NetLine/Plan by the Network Planning team.
Outcome
The final published schedule is distributed to all relevant systems and stakeholders, including Operations Control Duty Managers, SSIM, and Star Alliance partners, ensuring that all flight operations are accurately planned and executed.
