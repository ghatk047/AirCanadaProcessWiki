Bilateral and Regulatory Route Authority Filing

Onboarding a new codeshare or interline partner into schedule systems, covering system certification, designation testing and staff readiness before the first live booking.

Air Canada contextCommercial agreement and system readiness are frequently on different timelines, and the gap between a signed contract and a working, tested interface is where launch dates slip.
Trigger
A new codeshare or interline agreement is signed and requires system-level onboarding before routes can go on sale.
Outcome
A certified, tested partner interface with confirmed construction, through-check and staff readiness ahead of first sale.

Key performance indicators

  • All planned route pairs constructing successfully before go-live
  • Through-check tested at every relevant station before go-live
  • Configuration matching signed agreement terms at 100 percent
  • Go-live occurring on the committed date at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • Commercial launch dates announced before system readiness is actually confirmed
  • Test coverage missing a fare or cabin combination the agreement technically permits
  • Front-line staff learning about a new partner from a customer booking rather than a briefing
  • Station-level through-check testing not completing in time for a committed go-live date
