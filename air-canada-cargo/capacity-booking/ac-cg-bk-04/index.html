Key performance indicators

  • Average time to receive and log request: 6 minutes
  • Success rate of CBSA filings: 95%
  • Number of booking retries per process: 1.2
  • Average time to finalize booking: 12 minutes
  • Customer notification response time: 30 seconds

Risks and control points

  • System downtime during capacity checks can lead to missed bookings.
  • Errors in CBSA filing can result in legal penalties and delays.
  • Revenue accounting discrepancies due to incorrect data entry can impact financial reporting accuracy.
  • Failed customer notifications can cause confusion and operational delays.
  • Multiple booking retries can strain system resources and increase response times.
