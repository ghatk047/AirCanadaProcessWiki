Annual Budget and Operating Plan Build

The annual budget and operating plan are built, consolidating input from every business function into a single approved financial plan for the coming year.

Air Canada contextBudget consolidation is where every function's individual assumptions, from network capacity in AC-NP-SP-02 to crew establishment in AC-CM-CP-01, have to reconcile into one internally consistent financial plan.
Trigger
The annual budget cycle opens.
Outcome
An approved annual budget and operating plan, consolidated across all business functions and ready to serve as the year's financial baseline.
