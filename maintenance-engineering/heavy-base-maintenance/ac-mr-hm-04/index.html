Component Removal and Shop Visit Routing

A component is removed for shop visit, routed to the appropriate repair facility, and tracked through repair or overhaul back to a serviceable condition.

Air Canada contextComponent routing decisions balance repair cost, turnaround time and shop capability, and a component sent to the wrong facility or without correct documentation can lose weeks in transit and administrative correction rather than repair time itself.
Trigger
A component reaches its removal interval, or a defect requires its removal for shop repair.
Outcome
A component repaired or overhauled and returned to serviceable stock, correctly tracked through the full shop visit cycle.

Key performance indicators

  • Routing confirmed before shipment for 100 percent of removed components
  • Documentation complete for 100 percent of shipments at 100 percent
  • Tracked continuously against expected turnaround for 100 percent of open shop visits
  • Confirmed serviceable within 48 hours of receipt at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • Routing to a facility lacking the specific capability required, losing time in redirection
  • Incomplete documentation delaying a shop's ability to even begin work on receipt
  • A component received without complete certification documentation being unable to legally return to stock
  • Turnaround time overruns not being proactively flagged by the repair facility itself
