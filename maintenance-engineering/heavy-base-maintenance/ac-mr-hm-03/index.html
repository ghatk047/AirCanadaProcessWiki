Third Party MRO Vendor Management

Third-party MRO vendors performing heavy maintenance work on Air Canada's behalf are managed and governed against contracted service levels and quality standards.

Air Canada contextAir Canada remains ultimately accountable to Transport Canada for airworthiness regardless of who physically performs the work, which means vendor governance is a compliance control, not just a commercial contract management exercise.
Trigger
A heavy check or specific maintenance scope is contracted to a third-party MRO vendor.
Outcome
Vendor-performed work meeting Air Canada's quality and airworthiness standards, delivered within contracted timelines.

Key performance indicators

  • Agreed before work begins for 100 percent of contracted checks
  • On-site or remote oversight maintained for 100 percent of the check duration
  • Reviewed within the vendor's required response window at 95 percent
  • Confirmed before aircraft release for 100 percent of vendor checks

Risks and control points

  • Air Canada remaining accountable to Transport Canada regardless of which organisation performed the work
  • The depth of practical oversight at a vendor's own facility being inherently limited
  • A findings disposition approved too quickly under schedule pressure risking inadequate technical review
  • Scope ambiguity between Air Canada's expectation and the vendor's contract terms surfacing mid-check
