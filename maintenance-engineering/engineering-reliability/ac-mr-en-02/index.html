Airworthiness Directive Assessment and Embodiment

An airworthiness directive from Transport Canada or the design authority is assessed for applicability and embodied within its mandated compliance timeframe across the affected fleet.

Air Canada contextAn airworthiness directive is a mandatory regulatory instruction, not a recommendation, and the compliance timeframe is set by the regulator based on the safety risk the directive addresses, which makes this one of the least flexible deadlines anywhere in maintenance planning.
Trigger
Transport Canada or the type design authority issues an airworthiness directive.
Outcome
The directive assessed, embodied across the affected fleet and closed within its mandated compliance timeframe.

Key performance indicators

  • Assessed within the directive's initial review period at 100 percent
  • Planned to meet the mandated deadline for 100 percent of applicable tails
  • Embodied within the planned window for 95 percent of applicable tails
  • Compliance closed within the mandated deadline at 100 percent

Risks and control points

  • A directive not closed within its mandated deadline constituting a direct regulatory compliance failure
  • A very short compliance window requiring an unscheduled maintenance visit outside the normal cycle
  • Applicability assessment being ambiguous for a fleet with varying modification configurations
  • Embodiment planning competing for the same maintenance slots as every other scheduled work
