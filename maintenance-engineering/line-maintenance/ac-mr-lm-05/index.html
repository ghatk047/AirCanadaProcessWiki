Transit and Daily Check Execution

Transit and daily checks are performed within the turnaround or overnight ground time, confirming the aircraft's continued airworthiness for the next flight or flying day.

Air Canada contextTransit and daily checks are the routine backbone of line maintenance, and their reliability depends on being consistently performed within a ground time that also has to accommodate every other turnaround service, as covered in AC-GO-GT-01.
Trigger
An aircraft's scheduled transit or daily check interval comes due.
Outcome
A completed check confirming continued airworthiness, with the aircraft cleared for its next flight or flying day.

Key performance indicators

  • Scheduled within available ground time at 90 percent
  • Completed within the scheduled window at 90 percent
  • Certified before the required departure time at 95 percent
  • Check-related departure delay rate below target

Risks and control points

  • A check competing for the same ground time as every other turnaround service, risking being squeezed
  • A finding during the check extending it beyond its scheduled time under departure pressure
  • Certification depending on an authorised technician being physically available at completion time
  • Routine check reliability depending on consistency across a very high volume of daily occurrences
