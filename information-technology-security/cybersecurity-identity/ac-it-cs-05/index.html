Security Incident Response and Forensics

A confirmed security incident is contained, investigated and remediated, with forensic evidence preserved for potential regulatory or legal follow-up.

Air Canada contextSpeed and evidence preservation are frequently in tension during incident response, since the fastest path to containment can destroy forensic evidence a later investigation or regulatory inquiry would need.
Trigger
A security alert is escalated from triage in AC-IT-CS-03 into a confirmed security incident.
Outcome
The incident contained, root cause identified, systems remediated, and evidence preserved for any required follow-up.

Risks and control points

  • Scope determination needing to account for lateral movement beyond the initially detected point of entry
